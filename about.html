Our Story

From Humble Beginnings
To Global Excellence

KingFresh Trading Company was founded in 2019 in Mumbai, India, with a simple but powerful vision: to bring the exceptional quality of India's fresh produce to the world's tables. What started as a small export operation with 3 team members and a handful of farmers has grown into a globally recognized export powerhouse.

Today, we export to 50+ countries across 6 continents, handling over 10,000 metric tons of premium fresh fruits and vegetables annually. Our success is built on direct farmer partnerships, uncompromising quality control, and a passionate team dedicated to your satisfaction.

With Global GAP compliance and APEDA registration, we ensure every shipment meets the highest international standards — so you can trust KingFresh every time.

Our Journey

6 Years of Growth & Excellence

From a small export desk in Mumbai to a globally recognized brand — our journey has been extraordinary.

6+
Years of Uninterrupted Service
Serving global importers without a single year of disruption since 2019.
2019
Founded in Mumbai

KingFresh Trading Company established with 3 employees and partnerships with 12 mango farmers in Ratnagiri. APEDA registered from day one.

2020
First International Export

Secured our first export contracts to the UAE and UK — Alphonso mangoes and fresh onions — opening the Gulf and European markets.

2021
Product Range Expanded

Expanded our product range to include grapes, pomegranates, vegetables, and exotic produce — reaching 15+ export destinations.

2022
Cold Chain Infrastructure

Invested in state-of-the-art cold chain logistics and expanded to 20+ export markets across Asia, Africa, and Europe.

2023
Global GAP Certification

Achieved Global GAP certification across all partner farms. Reached 40+ country milestone with 500+ happy clients.

2025
50+ Countries & Beyond

Now exporting to 50+ countries with 10,000+ MT annually — and growing. Our best chapter is still being written.

Quality Standards

Our Quality Promise
