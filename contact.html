Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity?
Our standard minimum order is 1 metric ton (MT) per product. For air freight orders, we may accept smaller quantities. For sea freight, we recommend a minimum of 1 full container (around 18–22 MT) for best pricing.
Which countries do you export to?
We currently export to 50+ countries including UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Canada, USA, Singapore, Malaysia, Kenya, South Africa, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, and many more. Contact us to confirm availability to your country.
What certifications do your products have?
All our products come with Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, and Test Reports from NABL-accredited labs. Our company holds Global GAP certification and APEDA registration. We can provide all documents on request.
What payment terms do you accept?
We accept Letter of Credit (LC at sight), T/T (Telegraphic Transfer) — 30% advance + 70% against BL copy, and DP (Documents against Payment). For long-term clients, we also offer open terms after establishing a business relationship.
Can I get free samples before placing a bulk order?
Yes! For serious buyers, we offer free product samples via air courier. Sample quantity is typically 2–5 kg and the buyer pays only the courier cost. Contact us with your product interest and mailing address to arrange samples.
How long does delivery take?
Air freight: 1–3 days to most destinations. Sea freight: 7–25 days depending on the destination port. We provide real-time shipment tracking and proactive communication throughout the transit.
