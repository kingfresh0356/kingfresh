How It Works

Our Export Process

From farm to your warehouse — a seamless, transparent, and fully traceable journey.

🌾1

Farm Selection

We audit and select certified farms with proven track records of quality and sustainable practices.

🔬2

Quality Check

Multi-point inspection: size, Brix level, pesticide residue, and phytosanitary checks at source.

📦3

Grading & Packing

Automated grading lines pack produce in export-grade materials with customer-specified labeling.

🚢4

Freight & Export

Sea or air freight with full documentation: Phytosanitary, CoO, Bill of Lading, and more.

🏪5

Delivery & Support

Real-time tracking and dedicated after-delivery support. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Certifications
